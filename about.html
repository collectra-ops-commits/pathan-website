শুরুর গল্প — Origin

Why we started Pathan

A real problem, a real gap, and a decision to fix it.

Every year, thousands of Sylheti families send their children to Dhaka for university or work. Within weeks, the same story repeats — the food is wrong. Not bad, just not home. Not আম্মুর হাত। Not সাতকড়া। Not the hilsa the way only your mother makes it.

Families tried everything. Sending food by bus — risky and unreliable. Asking relatives to carry it — inconvenient. Sundarban or SA Paribahan — not designed for frozen food, no cold chain guarantee. The food arrived thawed, sometimes spoiled, always uncertain.

"There was no service that treated frozen homemade food as a serious logistics problem worth solving properly."

আমরা সিলেটি পরিবারের সেই সমস্যাটা নিজেদের চোখে দেখেছি। We saw it in our own families. Students in Dhanmondi going months without tasting Sylheti cooking. Parents in Sylhet packing containers of curry with no reliable way to send them. F-commerce home chefs with a loyal Dhaka customer base and no frozen delivery option.

So we built Pathan. Not a generic courier with a cooler bag. A dedicated sub-zero frozen food service — eutectic van, weekly batch model, door-to-door, Dhaka to Sylhet and back. Designed from the ground up for exactly this problem.

💡
The Problem — সমস্যা
Sylheti families with no reliable frozen food courier
Students in Dhaka, parents in Sylhet, home-cooked food with nowhere to go. Existing couriers had no cold chain for frozen items.
🔍
The Research — গবেষণা
Studying how frozen couriers work globally
We looked at models in India, Nigeria, the UK, and China. The eutectic van model — asset-light, batch-based — was the right answer for Bangladesh's infrastructure.
🚐
The Model — মডেল
Weekly batch delivery, eutectic van, no warehouse
One van. One route. One Friday night run. Rolling cold hub — no fixed warehouse costs. Lean, scalable, and reliable from day one.
🚀
The Launch — যাত্রা শুরু
Pathan goes live on the Dhaka–Sylhet route
Starting with one weekly run, a small team, and a clear mission — to make আম্মুর হাতের রান্না deliverable across any distance.

What Pathan stands for

Three commitments we will never compromise on.

প্রযুক্তি — Technology

The eutectic van —
our cold chain engine

We don't use a compressor van that needs the engine running. We use eutectic cold plate technology — the same used in pharmaceutical cold chains worldwide. Plates are pre-charged to −30°C before departure. They release cold steadily for 12–18 hours with no electricity needed on the road.


This means Dhaka traffic doesn't affect your food. A checkpoint delay doesn't raise the hold temperature. Your package arrives in Sylhet at the same −18°C it left Dhaka in.
