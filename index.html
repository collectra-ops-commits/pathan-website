কেন পাঠান — Why

Built for Bangladesh's families

Not another generic courier. Pathan is built specifically for frozen homemade food — with the cold chain to prove it.

🏠
Sender-to-Door Trust
প্রেরক থেকে দরজা পর্যন্ত বিশ্বাস

We collect directly from your home and deliver directly to your recipient's door. No third-party handoffs, no mystery warehouses, no broken cold chain.

❄️
True Sub-Zero Cold Chain
সত্যিকারের সাব-জিরো কোল্ড চেইন

Eutectic freezer technology maintains −18°C without electricity on the road. Your food arrives just as it left your freezer. Not chilled — frozen.

💛
আম্মুর হাতের রান্না
Your mother's cooking, not a substitute

We carry real homemade food — Satkara curry, Sylheti pithas, handmade hilsa preparations — not factory frozen meals. The original, from the original hands.
