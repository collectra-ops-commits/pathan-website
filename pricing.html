প্রশ্ন — FAQ

Pricing questions

When exactly do I get charged? ▼
Your deposit is only charged after the run is confirmed — meaning once enough bookings reach the 60% capacity threshold. If you book but the run doesn't confirm, your deposit is returned in full within 24 hours via the same payment method.
Is the price the same for both directions? ▼
Yes. The per-kg rate is the same whether you're sending Dhaka→Sylhet or Sylhet→Dhaka. The rate depends only on your service tier and weight.
What if my actual weight is different from what I estimated? ▼
Our rider will weigh your package at pickup. If the actual weight is higher than estimated, we'll send an adjusted deposit request via WhatsApp before confirming. If it's lower, we charge only the actual weight.
How do I qualify for Campus Box rate? ▼
Campus Box is a monthly subscription — you commit to booking at least one run per week for a month. Contact us on WhatsApp to set up your subscription. You'll get a fixed weekly pickup window and the lower ৳120/kg rate.
Are there any additional charges? ▼
No hidden charges. The per-kg rate covers pickup, cold chain transit, and last-mile delivery. The only variable is weight. No fuel surcharge, no zone fee, no packaging fee.
