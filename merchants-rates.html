চুক্তির শর্ত — Contract Terms

What the merchant contract covers

Simple, fair terms. No surprises.

📅
Contract Duration
চুক্তির মেয়াদ

Monthly contracts, auto-renewing each month. Cancel anytime with 2 weeks written notice via WhatsApp. No lock-in beyond the current month. No cancellation penalty.

🧾
Payment & Invoice
পেমেন্ট ও ইনভয়েস

Invoice sent at month end via WhatsApp with run-by-run breakdown. Payment due within 5 days via bKash, Nagad, or bank transfer. Late payment may suspend the slot.

📦
Minimum Volume
সর্বনিম্ন ভলিউম

Minimum 10 kg per run to maintain merchant rate. If volume drops below 10 kg for 2 consecutive runs without notice, the contract rate reverts to On-Demand for that period.

⏸️
Skipping a Week
সপ্তাহ বাদ দেওয়া

Skip any week with 48 hours notice by Wednesday 10 PM. No charge for skipped weeks. More than 2 skips per month without notice may trigger a contract review.
