প্রশ্ন — FAQ

Quick answers

How do I book a slot? ▼
Visit our Book page and fill in the form — it opens WhatsApp with your booking pre-filled. Or message us directly on WhatsApp at 01859 929 858. Bookings close every Thursday at 10 PM.
How do I track my order? ▼
Visit our Track page and enter your booking reference number and phone number. Your reference was sent to you on WhatsApp when your slot was confirmed.
I want to become a merchant partner — who do I contact? ▼
Visit our Merchant Application page and fill in the form. Or WhatsApp us at 01859 929 858 with your business name and estimated weekly volume. We respond within 48 hours.
My food arrived thawed — what do I do? ▼
Contact us on WhatsApp immediately — within 2 hours of delivery — with a photo of the issue. Message 01859 929 858 with your booking reference, the photo, and a brief description. We investigate every cold chain complaint and respond within the same day.
Can I cancel or change my booking? ▼
Yes — message us on WhatsApp before Wednesday 10 PM to cancel or change your booking for that week's run. After Wednesday 10 PM, cancellations may not be possible if the rider schedule is already set.
