প্রশ্ন — FAQ

Merchant questions

What is the minimum volume to qualify as a merchant? ▼
Minimum is 10 kg per run to qualify for the merchant rate and contract. If your volume is below 10 kg/week, the On-Demand or Campus Box tier is more appropriate.
Can I skip a week if I have no stock? ▼
Yes — with 48 hours notice. If you notify us by Wednesday 10 PM, your slot is paused for that week and you won't be invoiced. Repeated no-shows without notice may affect your contract.
Can I send in both directions — Dhaka to Sylhet and Sylhet to Dhaka? ▼
Yes. You can have separate contracts for each direction, or a combined contract if you regularly ship both ways. Each direction has its own pickup window and slot.
How does the monthly invoice work? ▼
At the end of each month we send you a WhatsApp invoice with a breakdown of each run — date, kg shipped, and total amount. Payment is due within 5 days via bKash, Nagad, or bank transfer.
How long is the minimum contract? ▼
Contracts are monthly, renewable automatically. You can cancel with 2 weeks notice. No lock-in beyond the current month.
